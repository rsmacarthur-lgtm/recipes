The master now holds **487 recipes**, up from 441. Alan and Steph's collection is untouched: all 441 original entries are present, and every cook's note they had written survives unedited. Everything added from Robert's side is prefixed **RM** and dated, so the two households stay separable.

Verified after the merge: all 441 originals present, all pre-existing notes intact, every harvested annotation traced into the final library, no duplicate entries introduced.

**46 recipes added** from Robert's Gmail archive. **9 recipes that were already in the master gained one of his annotations.** 23 recipes now carry an RM note. Across the whole collection, 198 of 487 entries carry a cook's note, up from 180.

### The Hawaiian Chicken Salad vinegar — no correction needed

The master's copy **already contains "1/2 c. white wine vinegar"**. It is the corrected version, credited to Jane Healy, matching the copy Robert's parents emailed on 23 May 2021 after the omission was caught.

Two details from the archive worth recording. The omission was caught by his brother **William**, not a friend, and what Will actually wrote was *"add in 1/2 cup white vinegar — Mom's secret ingredient"*; Steph corrected that the next day to *"1/2 c. white WINE vinegar to be exact."* And the corrected version differs from the 2012 one in two further places beyond the vinegar: the mustard line became "1 1/2 T. mustard (regular French's type)", and the macadamia quantity was dropped from "¾ cup, chopped toasted" to just "chopped, toasted macadamia nuts". The master carries the corrected wording throughout.

### The spatchcocked turkey note

It is the longest annotation in the archive and it is in verbatim, not summarised. The brief's recollection checks out with one correction: the thread says *"Dad says it's ok to take it out when the very thickest part of the breast is 150 next year"* — the word "Alan" never appears, so the note reads "Dad says". The 2015 entry (19 lb, convection 400°F, pulled at 149°F) is kept as the first line so the 149-versus-155 comparison is self-contained. The 2016 debrief also carries the apple pie finding, which is why the same observation appears on the Apple Pie entry.

## What I could not find, and other flags

- **Overnight White Bread.** Robert's April 2020 note says this is the version he now makes instead of the 2013 no-knead formula — but the recipe text exists nowhere. The only copy in the archive is a scanned book PDF attached to that email, ISBN 9781607742739, which is Ken Forkish's *Flour Water Salt Yeast*. The annotation is attached to *No-Knead Bread* so it is not orphaned, but the recipe he actually bakes is still missing from the collection. I did not transcribe it out of the book.
- **`recipez` is not a Gmail label.** It is a word in the subject line, and sometimes only in the body as a tag string like `rzbread rzlahey rzknead`. A label search returns nothing. About 201 threads match.
- **Deviled eggs** — the first-person commentary about seasoning to taste, the ziploc-piping trick and the Wickles-pickle garnish was in the recipe email itself rather than a reply. It is unmistakably Robert's own voice, so it was moved into the cook's note; the mashing and piping method stayed in the directions.
- **Four annotations came from a different thread than their recipe**: triple-cooked chips, saffron chicken, the Lahey stecca note, and the English muffins bake. Each entry's Further notes field says so.
- **Kung pao** — a 2014 text message reading "The kung pao was really hot. Just had it" was deliberately **not** used. It does not establish he cooked this recipe rather than ate takeout. Say the word and it goes in.
- **Lahey stecca was unbakeable as emailed** — the recipez copy stops after shaping with no baking step. Step 6 was completed from Robert's own fuller copy of the same text sent in May 2013.
- **Red-braised pork** reads "3.4 tsp salt", almost certainly 3/4. Left verbatim, flagged in Further notes.
- **Ad Hoc vinaigrette** — the emailed version says 2 Tbsp Dijon, the master says 1 1/2 tsp, a fourfold gap on one line. Everything else matches exactly. Nothing was changed; someone should decide which is right.
- **Six duplicate names remain**, all pre-existing in Alan and Steph's collection and none introduced here: Black Bean Salsa, Chili Dip, Greek Salad, Original Plum Torte, Panettone, Zucchini Gratin. Untouched, per the rule about not reorganising their entries.
- **Chicken salad 2022** has no ingredient list anywhere — the email is only ratios plus a link. It was entered as his ratios rather than inventing quantities.
- **Attributions left blank rather than guessed**: turmeric and saffron chicken (contextually Naz Deravian's *Bottom of the Pot*, never named in the emails), pan-fried risotto, cauliflower soup, fresh pasta, butterscotch pudding, and the stone-or-steel pizza crust.
